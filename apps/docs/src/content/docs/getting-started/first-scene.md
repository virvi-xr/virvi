After you sign up, the **Scenes** page shows a **Getting started** checklist with four steps. Each step ticks by itself once you've done it, so you can leave and come back. **Hide** removes the checklist, and it goes away by itself once all four are done.

## Create a scene

A scene is the content you run in a world: screens, images, models and sounds, arranged in presets (see [Core Concepts](/docs/getting-started/concepts/)).

On **Scenes**, choose one of these:

- **New scene** opens the scene builder. It asks where the scene goes (your Decentraland World, parcels, or other places such as Hyperfy), how big it is, what to start from (a theme, a room scan, or items you add), and how to arrange it.
- **Import scene** brings in a Decentraland scene you already have. See [Scene Import](/docs/dashboard/scene-import/).
- **Blank scene** makes an empty scene to build up yourself.

## Add something

Open the scene and add an item: a video screen, an image, a 3D model or a sound. Then place it, so it has a copy in the scene. An item with no copy placed doesn't show anywhere yet, so this step ticks once one is placed. You can upload your own files in the [Media Library](/docs/dashboard/media-library/), or use models from the catalog.

## Put it in a world

Open **Sync** to choose where the scene runs:

- **A Decentraland World or your parcels:** Virvi deploys the scene for you, or you sign each deploy yourself. A scene you build in your own project with [`@virvixr/dcl`](/docs/sdk/decentraland/install/) counts too, once it has been visited.
- **Hyperfy:** connect a world and drop the Virvi app into it. See [Hyperfy](/docs/sdk/hyperfy/install/).

## See it live

Visit your scene in the world. The step ticks once the scene reports in to Virvi from there: in Decentraland when someone loads it, and in Hyperfy when the world's Virvi app connects. From then on, changes you make in the dashboard show up in the world within seconds, and the in-world HUD lets you edit there too (see [In-World HUD](/docs/dashboard/in-world-hud/)).

You can try the Design Studio before you sign up. Go to `/try/studio` on virvi.io, describe what you want to make, and you're in. There's no account to create and no sign-in page. Virvi runs a quick automatic bot check in the background, and you don't need to do anything.

## What a guest can do

- Make up to **2 designs**.
- Send **3 free prompts** in total (not per day).
- Render and preview your designs.
- Upload small files, up to **25 MB** in total.
- Report a mistake if the Studio gets something wrong.

## What needs a free account

- Finalizing a design
- Downloads
- Top-ups
- Gift codes
- More designs or more prompts

When you hit one of these, the sign-up sheet opens right where you are. Once you've signed up, you carry on with what you were doing.

## When you sign up

Signing up keeps everything you made as a guest. Your account starts on the Free plan, which gives you 3 prompts a day.

If you sign in to an account you already have, the trial's designs move into that account.

## How long a trial lasts

A trial is kept until it has gone 14 days without use. The trial bar shows the date it will expire. Sign up before then to keep your designs.

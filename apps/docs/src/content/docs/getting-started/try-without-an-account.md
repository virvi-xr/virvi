You can try Virvi before you sign up. There are two ways in:

- **Design Studio:** go to `/try/studio` on virvi.io, describe what you want to make, and you're in.
- **Scene builder:** go to `/try/scene` (or follow "Build a scene — no account needed" on the home page) and press **Start building**.

There's no account to create and no sign-in page. Virvi runs a quick automatic bot check in the background, and you don't need to do anything. Both ways in start the same trial, so as a guest you can use everything below.

## What a guest can do

### Design Studio

- Make up to **2 designs**.
- Send **3 free prompts** in total (not per day).
- Render and preview your designs.
- Upload small files, up to **25 MB** in total.

### Scene builder

- Walk every step of the new scene wizard up to Deploy: Where, Size, Assets, Arrange and the 3D preview.
- Browse the public catalog and place its models.
- Start from one of your own room scans (see below).

Your layout is kept in this browser while you work, so a reload doesn't lose it.

### Your scenes

- Open the scenes your trial holds (an import makes one), rename them and edit them: presets, and models, images, video and sound.
- Other element types, room sets and the account-only scene tabs (roles, collaborators, destinations, Hyperfy, streams, showings, schedules, AR) need an account.

### Import

- Upload a Creator Hub or SDK7 scene zip, up to **25 MB**, **2 imports** per trial (a failed one counts).
- Virvi checks it, adds Virvi to it and builds it. You see the import page (name, place, thumbnail, build log) and can open its Virvi scene to edit.
- A guest's import is kept apart from the real World or parcels it names: it never changes anyone's live scene until an account deploys it.

### Room scans

- Pair a personal headset with the usual `/pair` code, then choose **Scan this room** in the Virvi Quest app. You don't need to be building in a space.
- Your trial holds up to **5 room scans** at once. Pick one in the wizard to preview the room Virvi makes from it.
- Scanning as a guest needs the latest Virvi Quest app. If **Scan this room** isn't on the builder panel's space list, update the app.

### Demos

Analytics, Events and Giveaways open as demos, marked **Demo** in the sidebar:

- **Analytics:** a week of visits to the sample World `virvi-sample.dcl.eth`, with its heatmap, visitors and sessions.
- **Events:** a sample schedule on the sample scene.
- **Giveaways:** a sample giveaway with items and claims.

Demo with sample data. Changes stay in this browser and are never sent anywhere. You can create, edit and delete events and giveaways to see how they work; your changes are gone when the trial ends or you sign out.

## What needs a free account

- Finalizing a design, downloads, top-ups, gift codes, and more designs or prompts
- Saving a scene from the wizard, and deploying to a Decentraland World, Hyperfy or Virvi AR
- Deploying an import, downloading its backup or live files, a third import, or a zip over 25 MB
- More than 5 room scans, shared headsets, and making a library asset from a scan
- Anything real behind the demos: your own analytics, publishing events, real giveaways and claims, streaming
- Media uploads, settings, billing, organizations and everything else

When you hit one of these, the sign-up sheet opens right where you are. If you sign up or sign in with email or a wallet, what you were doing carries on by itself. With Google or Discord, you come back to the page you were on and a note tells you which button to press again.

## Deploying creates your account

You don't have to sign up before you deploy. In the wizard's Where step, choose "Decentraland World — you'll connect the wallet that owns it when you deploy." At the Deploy step:

1. "Connect your wallet to deploy. This also creates your Virvi account." Connecting signs you in with that wallet: a new wallet becomes your account, and a wallet that already has an account takes your trial's work.
2. Virvi lists that wallet's Worlds. Pick one (or it's picked for you when there's only one with no scene on it).
3. "Check your wallet: sign once to create your account, then once more to deploy."

If the wallet doesn't own a Decentraland NAME, you'll see: "This wallet doesn't own a Decentraland NAME. Your account is ready: connect the wallet that owns your World, or pick Hyperfy or Virvi AR instead."

For Hyperfy or Virvi AR, **Create and deploy** opens the sign-up sheet: "Create a free account to save *your scene*. Your layout is kept." Once you've signed up, the deploy carries on.

Deploying an import works the same way: the import page asks you to connect your wallet, which creates your account, and then deploys it.

## When you sign up

Signing up keeps everything you made as a guest: designs, scenes, imports, room scans and paired headsets. Your account starts on the Free plan, which gives you 3 prompts a day.

If you sign in to an account you already have, the trial's work moves into that account.

## How long a trial lasts

A trial is kept until it has gone 14 days without use. The trial bar shows the date it will expire. Sign up before then to keep your work.

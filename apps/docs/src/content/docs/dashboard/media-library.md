---
title: Media Library and 3D Asset Library
description: Where your images, video and 3D models live, and who can see what you upload.
---

The dashboard has two libraries. They stay separate.

| | **Media Library** (Media) | **3D Asset Library** (Assets) |
|---|---|---|
| For | Images and video for your scenes | 3D models, your own and shared ones |
| Holds | PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF (up to 10 MB) and MP4/WebM video (up to 70 MB). No 3D models. | GLB models (up to 50 MB) only |
| New uploads | Listed for you only | Private: listed for you only, under **My Assets** |

## Media Library

Upload images and video on the **Media** page, or from a scene's image, video and ad fields. Only you see your files in the list. Once you place a file in a scene, the scene's visitors see it there.

The Media Library doesn't take 3D models: a GLB or glTF file is refused with a pointer to the 3D Asset Library. GLBs you uploaded to Media before this change stay there and keep working in your scenes.

## 3D Asset Library

The **Assets** page has two views:

- **All Assets:** shared models everyone can use, including Virvi's free [catalog packs](/dashboard/catalog-packs/).
- **My Assets:** your own models, public and private: your uploads, models made from a room scan, and pieces you finalize in the Design Studio.

New uploads are private: only you see them in the library, and you can place them in your own scenes. On a scene's model element, **Upload GLB** adds the model to your 3D Asset Library the same way.

## Who can open a file

Files in both libraries are stored at long, unguessable links. "Private" and "listed for you only" mean the file isn't listed for anyone else in these libraries. But **anyone with the link can open it**, a scene's editors see the files added to that scene, and its visitors see what's placed in it. Don't upload anything that must stay secret.

## A scene's library

Each scene also has its own library: the files and models added to that scene, ready to place. The in-world HUD shows it as the **Media Library** panel. It lists what's been added to that scene: images placed from your Media Library, models added from the 3D Asset Library with **Add to scene**, and files uploaded from in-world. See [In-world HUD](/dashboard/in-world-hud/#uploading-from-in-world).

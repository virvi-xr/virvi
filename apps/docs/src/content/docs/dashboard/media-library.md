The dashboard has two libraries. They stay separate.

| | **Images & Video** | **3D Models** |
|---|---|---|
| For | Images and video for your scenes | 3D models, your own and shared ones |
| Holds | PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF (up to 10 MB) and MP4/WebM video (up to 70 MB). No 3D models. | GLB models (up to 50 MB) only |
| New uploads | Listed for you only | Private: listed for you only, under **My Models**, until you share them |

## Images & Video

Upload images and video on the **Images & Video** page, or from a scene's image, video and ad fields. Only you see your files in the list. Once you place a file in a scene, the scene's visitors see it there.

Images & Video doesn't take 3D models: a GLB or glTF file is refused with a pointer to 3D Models. GLBs uploaded here before the two libraries split have moved to your 3D Models, private to you. Their files and links didn't change, so every scene that uses them keeps working.

## 3D Models

The **3D Models** page has three views:

- **All Models:** shared models everyone can use, including Virvi's free [catalog packs](/docs/dashboard/catalog-packs/).
- **Community:** models other Virvi users shared, credited to them.
- **My Models:** your own models, public and private: your uploads, models uploaded from in-world, models made from a room scan, and pieces you finalize in the Design Studio.

New uploads are private: only you see them in the library, and you can place them in your own scenes. On a scene's model element, **Upload GLB** adds the model to your 3D Models the same way, and **Choose from 3D Models** picks one of yours.

### Sharing a model

Open one of your models and press **Share with the community**. Pick a license, say how you'd like to be credited (your name if you leave it empty), and confirm that you made the model or have the right to share it. It then shows in **Community** and in All Models, and anyone can add it to their scenes.

| License | What other people may do with it |
|---|---|
| CC0 (public domain) | Use, change and sell it, for anything, without crediting you |
| CC BY 4.0 | Use, change and sell it, as long as they credit you |
| CC BY-SA 4.0 | As CC BY, and anything made from it is shared under the same license |
| CC BY-ND 4.0 | Use it as it is, commercially too, with credit; no sharing changed versions |
| CC BY-NC 4.0 | Use and change it with credit, but not to make money: no ticketed or sponsored events, sales or ads |
| CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 | As CC BY-NC, and anything made from it is shared under the same license |
| CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 | Use it as it is, with credit, not to make money, no changed versions |
| Virvi users only | Place it in Virvi scenes, events included, with credit; not to be taken elsewhere or resold |

**Make private** stops new people adding it. Scenes that already have it keep it, and Creative Commons licenses can't be taken back from people who already have a copy. Deleting a model that someone else's scene uses works the same way: it leaves your library and stays, private, for those scenes. Models made from a room scan can't be shared: they show the layout of a home.

Anyone signed in can **Report** a model someone shared (a copyright claim, something offensive, private information, a broken file or spam). Virvi support reviews reports and can take a model down: it stops being shared, and its owner can't share it again.

## Who can open a file

Files in both libraries are stored at long, unguessable links. "Private" and "listed for you only" mean the file isn't listed for anyone else in these libraries. But **anyone with the link can open it**, a scene's editors see the files added to that scene, and its visitors see what's placed in it. Don't upload anything that must stay secret.

## A scene's library

Each scene also has its own library: the models and images added to that scene, ready to place. The in-world HUD shows it as the **3D Models** panel. It lists what's been added to that scene: images placed from Images & Video, models added from 3D Models with **Add to scene**, and files uploaded from in-world. Models uploaded from in-world go in the scene owner's 3D Models, private (a renter's go in their own); images go in Images & Video. See [In-world HUD](/docs/dashboard/in-world-hud/#uploading-from-in-world).

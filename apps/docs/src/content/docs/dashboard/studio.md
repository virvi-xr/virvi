**Studio** in the dashboard's sidebar holds your scenes and the things the Design Studio makes for you. It opens on the **Scenes** tab.

## Tabs

- **Scenes:** your scenes. Start a new one or import one from here.
- **Wearables:** your wearable designs.
- **Emotes:** your emote designs.
- **Buildings:** your building designs.
- **Decor:** your decor designs: furnishings and other pieces to place in a scene.
- **All designs:** every design, whatever it is.

The design tabs show only where the Design Studio is available, and in a guest trial. On a self-hosted install, Studio has the **Scenes** tab alone.

**New design** on a design tab starts that kind of design. You can pick another kind before you create it: Wearable, Emote, Building or Decor.

A design you're invited to shows in its tab under **Shared with me**.

## Building or decor

A building or decor design is told apart by what its agent makes. Once it has pieces, a design with a building in it is under **Buildings**, and one with only decor pieces is under **Decor**. Before it has any pieces, it stays where you created it.

## Linking to a tab

`/scenes` opens the **Scenes** tab, `/studio` opens **All designs**, and `/studio?kind=wearable`, `emote`, `building` or `decor` opens that tab.

**Collection** (under Tools) lists what your linked wallets hold in Decentraland:

- **Wearables:** with their slot and rarity.
- **Emotes.**
- **Names:** your `.dcl.eth` names.
- **Land:** parcels and estates.

It covers items on both Ethereum and Polygon. Each one links to its page on the Decentraland Marketplace.

## Using it

- **Filter by kind:** Everything, Wearables, Emotes, Names or Land. Each filter shows how many you have.
- **Search** by name.
- **Pick one wallet** when more than one is linked.
- **Order:** the rarest items come first.

Only wallets you've linked and confirmed in **Settings** count. The page shows your own wallets only, and nobody else can see it.

## Where the data comes from

Virvi reads the Decentraland Marketplace for each linked wallet. It keeps what it found for 5 minutes, so something you just bought may take a few minutes to show. If the Marketplace doesn't answer for a wallet, the page says so and shows the rest. It lists up to 2,000 items per wallet, newest first, and says so when a wallet holds more.

title: Cities
description: The Virvi-run Cities, and where to visit them.
---

A **City** is a world run by Virvi, with apartments, stores and venues to look around.

Open **Cities** in the dashboard's sidebar to see them. The same list is public at [virvi.io/cities](https://virvi.io/cities), so you can share it with people who don't have an account. A guest trial can open Cities too.

Each City shows its name, a short description and one link per world:

- **Visit in Decentraland** (or Hyperfy, or AR) opens the City there in a new tab.
- **Coming to Hyperfy** (or another platform) means that world isn't open yet.

A City with at least one world open is marked **Live**.

Today there is one City, **Virvi City**, live in Decentraland at the World `virvicity.dcl.eth` and coming to Hyperfy.
